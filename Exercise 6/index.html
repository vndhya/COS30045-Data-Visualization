How much energy do TVs consume?

This histogram groups TV models by their labelled annual energy consumption. Each bar shows the number of models within an energy consumption range. Select a screen technology to filter the histogram.

Energy consumption and star ratings

Each dot represents a TV model. Its position shows its star rating and labelled annual energy consumption. Colours identify its screen technology.
