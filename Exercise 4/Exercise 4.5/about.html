About Us

This website is a student project exploring appliance energy consumption in the Australian market. It was created for COS30045 Data Visualisation at Swinburne University of Technology.

Our purpose

The aim is to present appliance energy information in a clear and accessible way. This first version provides the foundation for interactive charts and data comparisons in later exercises.
